SANDBOX VR

LIMBS TRACKER

A modular wearable system for VR limb trackers that simplifies gear-up while delivering a secure fit, stable tracker positioning, and consistent tracking performance throughout active gameplay.

Problem


VR limb trackers can be difficult to use because they often require time for setup, may experience orientation errors that affect tracking accuracy, need proper fitting to stay secure and comfortable under high impact gameplay, and require regular cleaning to maintain hygiene and performance.

Various types of securing mechanisms were explored throughout the design process. Each concept was evaluated for ease of use, security, comfort, durability, and its ability to maintain consistent tracker positioning during active VR gameplay.

Modularity

The same wrist/ankle straps accommodate both left- and right-side variants :
Left wrist (L)  /  Left ankle (L)  /  Right wrist (R)  /  Right ankle (R)

Single wrist and ankle strap designs are used throughout, mounting in opposite orientations, onto the same tracker base. This mirrored installation enables full left/right compatibility with single strap designs, which also minimises production and storage costs.

Adjustment

The wide adjustment range accommodates a variety of wrist sizes, making the strap suitable for both children and adults. This adaptable design provides a personalized fit without the need for multiple strap sizes.

Magnets arrangement

Most magnetic watch straps currently on the market can only be used in a specific orientation, users cannot secure the strap end if it is too long.

To address this limitation, magnets with alternating polarity are integrated into the strap, enabling intuitive and precise length adjustment, while maintaining a secure, comfortable, and easy-to-use fit.

Gear up

Both straps follow a simple interaction flow – wear > pull to tighten > secure – creating an intuitive and efficient user experience.

The wrist tracker strap is designed to form a loop, enabling users to put the tracker on with single hand, without the assistance from store staff.

The ankle tracker strap is designed to be worn on the outside of ankle, allowing full range of motion and natural bending during gameplay.

Protective flaps

Protective flaps on both wrist and ankle straps shield the magnetic fasteners from catching on other gear. Working alongside an array of strong magnets, the flaps provide an additional layer of protection that enhances fastening reliability, reducing the risk of accidental loosening during high intensity and fast paced gameplay.

CMF selection

Silicone was selected for its durability and flexibility, enabling the trackers to maintain a secure fit while resisting wear, cracking, and deformation over time.

Its water-resistant properties and ease of cleaning make it ideal for wearable applications, where sweat and dirt can accumulate during regular use. This ensures the trackers remain hygienic and suitable for repeated use.

In addition to its practical advantages, silicone provides a soft, comfortable surface against the skin, enhancing the overall user experience. A dark grey matte finish was chosen to create a premium aesthetic while allowing the player colours to stand out clearly.

Branding

A raised logo on the underside of the device temporarily leaves an imprint on player’s wrists after use. This creates a memorable interaction with the product while reinforcing brand identity in a subtle and playful way.
