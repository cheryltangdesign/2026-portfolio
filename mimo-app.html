MIMO APP

MIMO UI ecosystem consists of products, including –
MIMO phone, MIMO watch and parent’s phone.

The Ecosystem

By linking between various platforms – interactive hardware and devices, children can instantly and
seamlessly share their happy moments with their parents and buddies.

Also the health tracker monitors statistics like heart rate and sport duration, parents can set
personalised exercise goals for their kids. Integrating with the reward system to help their kids form
regular and healthy habits.

Instant sharing

Media such as selfies, photos and voice messages can be shared via a single button,
letting young users connect with their parents and buddies with an easier and faster experience.

Health tracker

Customisable challenges promote healthier lifestyles for the kids.

Different combinations of sport types and durations determine the challenge’s difficulty and amount
of star reward the kid receives. The young users will be rewarded with stars upon completing the
exercise, with the aid of the MIMO devices.

The star reward system can be exchanged as an increase of screen time or monetary reward, and
help form good habits through monetary and non-monetary incentives.
