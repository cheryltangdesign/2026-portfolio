NETFLIX HOUSE

NETFLIX V/RTUALS

Netflix V/RTUALS – Developed in partnership with Netflix, creating immersive free-roam VR experiences
that let visitors physically and virtually interact with iconic worlds from popular Netflix series, including
Stranger Things, Squid Game, and Rebel Moon.

Back-to-back sessions

Two independent sets of VR equipment (Gear A / Gear B) are setup within a single arena (Stage 1 / Stage 2) to
support back-to-back sessions. This setup improves operational and cost efficiency while preventing
software-linked equipment from being mixed between sessions.

White for Stage 1 and black for Stage 2, visual identifiers such as stickers, velcro patches, and 3D printed
attachments are added to existing Sandbox VR equipment to help staff quickly and easily distinguish
between the two sets of gear.
